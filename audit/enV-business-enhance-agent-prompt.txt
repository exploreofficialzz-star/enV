# PART 1 — USER REVIEWS & COMPLAINTS (design against these)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Wave / full invoicing platforms (Reddit, Trustpilot 2025–2026)
Users repeatedly report:
- Payment holds and delayed deposits (weeks) with weak support
- Trust collapse when money is “shown paid” but not in bank
- Free-tier erosion (features moving behind Pro)
- MFA lockouts, bot support, hard to reach humans
- Fear of putting business financial life in one vendor

**enV response:** Never hold money. Never store invoice client data on servers.
Position: “Generate the document locally; you email/PDF it yourself.”

## One-off invoice generators
Complaints: watermarks, signup walls, ads, unclear privacy, thin line-item UX.

**enV response:** No watermark, no signup, privacy banner, multi line-items, currency, local logo.

## Name generators (Namelix / Shopify-class user feedback)
- 40%+ of “good” names already taken as .com
- Domain “available” sometimes means premium $800+
- No trademark check (users still get burned)
- Vague keywords → repetitive / generic output
- Logo upsell funnels
- Keyword+Labs/Works style feels cheap

**enV response:**
- Multi-style local combinators (not 6 fixed templates)
- Volume (24–48 names) + style tabs
- Strong disclaimer: no TM, domain, social, or cultural guarantee
- Optional “check domain” = external registrar link only (no fake green checks)
- No logo upsell

## LTV/CAC calculator users
Want: correct labels, **health bands**, payback months, plain-English “what to do,”
not a naked number.

**enV response:** Finance Studio modes with specialist labels + benchmarks.
