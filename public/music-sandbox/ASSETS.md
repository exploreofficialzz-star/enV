# Sandbox assets

- `rumuo-mark.jpg` is the Rumuo icon supplied by the user. `lora.ttf` was extracted from the supplied `splash-screen-design-spec.pdf` / embedded `design-spec.json`.
- The enV logo uses the existing homepage light and dark assets in `public/`.
- `inter.ttf` and `aclonica.ttf` were retrieved through the Google Fonts CSS API, family names Inter and Aclonica, for local hosting. Their licenses are retained alongside these files.
- The headset and interface icons use the project's existing `lucide-react` dependency.
- The brand images retain their original colors against the monochrome interface.
